Terms of use

Short, in plain language. Using the bot means you agree with what is below.

What the service is

Grailert is a notification tool. It monitors public listings on Vinted according to the search parameters you provide and sends you a message when something matching appears. It is an independent product, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Vinted.

What it is not

It is not a guarantee that you will find, win or resell anything. Availability of listings, prices and the behaviour of the marketplace are outside our control. The service is provided as-is, without a promise of uninterrupted operation.

Your account elsewhere

Grailert never accesses your Vinted account and never asks for its credentials. You remain responsible for how you use the marketplace itself, including its own terms.

Payment

Paid plans are prepaid for a fixed period through Telegram Stars or Crypto Pay. There is no stored payment method and no automatic renewal: when the period ends, the plan simply stops. Underpaid invoices are not treated as payment. Questions about a specific payment go to the bot, which can show you its status.

Fair use

Plans are personal. Reselling access, sharing one plan across many people, or using the service to overload the marketplace will end the plan without a refund.

Ending the service

You can stop at any time by not renewing; there is nothing to cancel. We may suspend an account that abuses the service, and we may stop offering the product entirely — in which case any unused prepaid period is refundable on request.
