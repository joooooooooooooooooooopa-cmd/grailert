Your Vinted account stays yours

This page exists because it is the single most important thing to understand before installing any tool around a marketplace that bans people for automation.

What Vinted actually punishes

Vinted's terms forbid external software tools, bots and scrapers, and the platform enforces that with a bot-detection layer. But enforcement lands where it can be proven: on actions performed from a user's own account.

  • automatic buying the moment a listing appears
  • re-listing your own items on a schedule to stay on top of the feed
  • bulk messaging buyers or sellers
  • anything driven by a logged-in session that a human could not physically perform

The February 2026 wave, reported as a roughly 340% increase in bans, hit that behaviour. A further round of restrictions followed in July 2026 aimed at automated re-listing.

The honest part

We are not going to claim that a marketplace can never change its mind about third-party tools in general. What we can say precisely is this: there is no action taken on your account, so there is nothing on your account to detect, attribute or punish. If Grailert ever stopped working, the failure would be ours — a blocked server address — and not a mark on your profile.

That is also why the product deliberately lacks auto-buy. It is the most requested feature in this category and the one that gets customers banned. We would rather be the tool you can keep using.

What we store about you

Your Telegram id and username, your language, your plan, your saved searches and which listings were already sent so you do not get them twice. No Vinted credentials, because we never have any. Details on the privacy page.
