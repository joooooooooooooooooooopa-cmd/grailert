Privacy

The short version: we store what is needed to run your searches and your plan, and nothing about your Vinted account, because we never have access to it.

What we store

  • From Telegram: your numeric id, your username if you have one, and your language setting. We never see your phone number.
  • Your searches: the name you gave them, the search link or keyword, the check interval and your word filters.
  • Delivery bookkeeping: identifiers of listings already sent, so the same item does not arrive twice.
  • Payments: which plan, which method, amount, currency, status and the invoice identifier. Card and wallet details never reach us — they stay with Telegram or Crypto Pay.

What we never store

Vinted credentials, because there is no login. Payment credentials. Your contacts, your other chats, or anything outside the conversation with the bot.

Where it goes

Data stays on our server. The bot talks to Telegram (to deliver messages), to Vinted (to read public listings), to Crypto Pay (for invoices) and to a European Central Bank rate feed (to convert prices). There are no advertising or analytics trackers.

How long

For as long as you use the bot. Delete a search and its data goes with it; ask the bot to remove your account and everything is deleted, payments aside — those are kept as financial records.

Your requests

Write to the bot to get a copy of your data or to have it deleted.
