Bans and account safety

Can Grailert get my Vinted account banned?
Nothing we do is attached to your account. Grailert reads the public catalogue the same way an anonymous visitor does, and writes to your Telegram. It never logs in, never buys, never posts, never messages sellers. There is no action on your account for Vinted to flag.

Then why do people get banned for using tools?
Because most tools act as you: auto-buying, re-listing on a schedule, bulk messaging. Those actions come from your account and are trivially detectable. The February 2026 wave hit exactly that behaviour. Read the longer version on the account safety page.

Do you need my Vinted password?
No — and we would refuse it if you offered. There is no login step anywhere in the product.

How it works

Which countries are covered?
Twelve: Poland, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, the United Kingdom, Czechia, Lithuania, Portugal, Belgium and Slovakia.

Can I use my own filters?
Yes, and it is the better way. Set your filters on Vinted — brand, size, condition, price — copy the address bar link and send it to the bot. It will watch exactly that query. A plain keyword works too if you want the whole site.

How fast is it really?
On Pro the check runs every 30 seconds and the alert reaches your phone within a second of that check. In practice you see a listing before it scrolls off the first row of the app.

Will it spam me?
No. A new search is silently baselined: the bot remembers what is already listed and only sends what appears afterwards. If a burst arrives, at most eight alerts are sent and the rest come as one summary line.

Money

How do I pay?
Telegram Stars or crypto (USDT, TON, BTC and others) through Crypto Pay. Crypto is listed first because Stars lose roughly a third to app-store fees on mobile purchases.

Is there automatic renewal?
No. No card is stored and nothing renews itself. When the period ends, the plan stops.

What happens to my searches when a plan ends?
They are paused, never deleted. Renew and they pick up where they left off.

Refunds?
Ask in the bot. There is a free plan and a seven-day Pro trial specifically so that nobody has to pay to find out whether the tool works for them.
